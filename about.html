About CertSmart

Smarter Compliance.Stronger Confidence.

A certificate of insurance is a snapshot of coverage—it doesn’t prove a contract’s requirements are met. Only a review of the actual policies and endorsements can.

CertSmart is a proprietary platform for insurance verification, contract analysis and certificate management. It helps organizations prepare, manage and track certificates that hold up to the contract.
